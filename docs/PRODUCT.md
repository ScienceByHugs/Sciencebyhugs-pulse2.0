# Pulse 2.0 Product Direction

Pulse 2.0 is a personal protocol operating system.

## Product rule
Logging something in Pulse should take less effort than remembering it.

## Launch pillars
1. Today screen
2. Two-tap Quick Log
3. Protocol scheduling
4. Automatic inventory tracking
5. Site rotation
6. Native notifications
7. Supply forecasting
8. Timeline and calendar
9. Calculator suite
10. Privacy and Face ID
11. Apple Health
12. Offline-first behavior

## Safety boundary
Pulse tracks and calculates from user-entered information. It does not prescribe, diagnose, or recommend treatment or dosage.
